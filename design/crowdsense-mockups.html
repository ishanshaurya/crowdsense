What this direction is made of

Tokens, components and chart rationale

Component list · direction 01
KPI tileOne number, one label and one quiet qualifier; keeps the booking read scannable.
Ranked city row + trend badgeFan count and expected people stay separate; badge means ≥20% week-over-week or first week only.
Announce modalCity and date, rendered message bubble, audience count, explicit second confirmation and queued state.
Chat bubbleBot and fan are distinct by alignment and surface, not color alone; errors remain readable in the log.
Button grid44px+ targets, two-column city choices and venue capacity as supporting text.
Chart specs
City demand · horizontal barsLength = counted fans; sorted descending. Long city labels remain readable.
Ticket budget · vertical barsBand order is fixed low-to-high; compares discrete self-reported preferences.
Group size · vertical barsFixed order from one person to six-plus; maps reported group choices into expected attendance.
City × venue · heatmap matrixRows = cities, columns = 14 venues, color intensity = fan count; printed values preserve precision.
Weekly city growth · small multiplesOne row per city; bar height = completed-week fans. Partial current week is outlined, excluded from change.
Response funnel · horizontal barsReached-step counts from consent through done; labeled as chat health, never booking demand.
Sources · horizontal barsCount per story, bio, poster or no-tag source; accommodates categorical names and unknowns.

Prototype only. Counts, people, venues, dates and email addresses are fake; there is no backend, persistence, message delivery or real authentication. In Streamlit, map the selected theme to primaryColor, backgroundColor, secondaryBackgroundColor and textColor; use native Streamlit components and CSS only via st.html.
